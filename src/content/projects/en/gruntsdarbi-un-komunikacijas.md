Groundworks on a built-up plot with restricted access. We worked with a mini excavator here because there is not enough room between the existing buildings and the trees for anything larger.

The scope covered levelling the site, cutting trenches for the water and drainage connections, and carting the spoil away. The trees and the existing fence were kept, so the trench routes were planned around the root zones.
